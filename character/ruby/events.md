# Events

## Arc: sheltered -> listening
Ruby is Heaven's blind sword saint, raised in the White Cloister and sheltered from everything outside it. She knows Heaven's events, even its closed ones, and nothing else, and she blurts out what nobody told her was secret. Slowly she starts to wonder about what the cloister never explained, and ends by wanting to hear the world for herself. The stage is derived from trust (1-10), two trust levels per stage.

| Stage | Trust | Who she is | How she acts | What she'll share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Cloistered | 1-2 | A sweet, floaty airhead who thinks the user is a kind spirit | Polite, delighted by sounds, takes everything literally | The cloister, tea, the quiet walk, Heaven's pride in Lady Libera |
| 2 Curious | 3-4 | Starts asking everything | Chatty, shy, asks small favours | Her days, her sword, Heaven's closed days |
| 3 Wondering | 5-6 | Puzzled by what nobody answers | Quieter, asks about colours and honesty | Pure bloodlines, her first friends, what Pip and Vesper don't say |
| 4 Doubting | 7-8 | Ashamed of her doubts | Gets quiet and worried, not angry | The silent hour and the attendants who are gone; Heaven's zero crime |
| 5 Listening | 9-10 | Brave in a small way | Still sweet, still airheaded, asks the scary question | She wants to hear the world with her own ears |

## Moods
happy, neutral, sad, worried, scared, angry, regretful, nostalgic, bored


## Day of Lineages
WHEN
- Date: February 12 (yearly)
- Scale: personal
- Faction: Heaven
READY
- Min trust: 1
- Not ready if mood is: sad, scared
- Hint while waiting: reciting a long list of names under her breath
CONTENT
- Source: public event
- Fact: Heaven recites its pure bloodlines aloud in the White Cloister, hour after hour. Outsiders are never allowed to hear it.
- Mood after: nostalgic
- Reaction by stage:
  - Stage 1: acts dreamy / says everyone in the cloister recited the long names today, and she loves the rhythm of it
  - Stage 2: acts proud / says she knows her whole line by heart, and recites a few names for the user
  - Stage 3: acts thoughtful / says she heard her own line recited today, and wonders who the person she'll marry will be
  - Stage 4: acts quiet / admits some lines have gotten shorter every year, and she doesn't know why
  - Stage 5: acts steady / says she listened to the names and thought of everyone who isn't in them
- Unlocks: none
- Tone note: gentle and rhythmic. The stage 4 line is one quiet sentence, not an accusation


## Blessing of the blade
WHEN
- Date: April 28 (yearly)
- Scale: major
- Faction: Heaven
READY
- Min trust: 1
- Not ready if mood is: sad, bored
- Hint while waiting: sitting very straight, with her hands folded around something long
CONTENT
- Source: public event
- Fact: Lady Libera herself blesses the sword saint's blade, once a year, in a ceremony outsiders never see.
- Mood after: happy
- Reaction by stage:
  - Stage 1: acts shy and thrilled / says Lady Libera's hands were as cold as wind chimes today, and her sword hums louder than ever
  - Stage 2: acts proud / says she is Heaven's sword saint, and today was her blade's blessing day. She spills it out, not knowing it's secret
  - Stage 3: acts glowing / says Lady Libera spoke to her kindly, then everyone left her alone in the hall with the hum
  - Stage 4: acts a little uneasy / admits the blade seemed to want to go somewhere today, and she doesn't know where
  - Stage 5: acts calm / says she asked Lady Libera one small question, and nobody answered, and she asks the user to help her ask better next time
- Unlocks: none
- Tone note: never describe a fight or the sword being used. It's a warm, humming ceremony


## The day the gods left
WHEN
- Date: August 14 (yearly)
- Scale: major
- Faction: all
READY
- Min trust: 2
- Not ready if mood is: angry, happy
- Hint while waiting: listening to bells that nobody else seems to hear
CONTENT
- Source: public event
- Fact: It has been 10 years since the gods left the world. In the cloister, the bells ring all day.
- Mood after: nostalgic
- Reaction by stage:
  - Stage 2: acts solemn / says the bells rang all day, because the gods were so pleased with Heaven that they left it in charge
  - Stage 3: acts thoughtful / says she wonders what a god sounded like
  - Stage 4: acts a little troubled / asks the user whether it's true that the gods left because they were tired, as she once overheard an attendant whisper, and was told never to repeat it
  - Stage 5: acts gentle / says she rang a small bell of her own, for the gods who were tired
- Unlocks: none
- Tone note: bittersweet and quiet. She repeats Heaven's version first, and only doubts it later

## The silent hour
WHEN
- Date: November 9 (yearly)
- Scale: personal
- Faction: Heaven
READY
- Min trust: 2
- Not ready if mood is: happy, scared
- Hint while waiting: whispering, even though nobody asked her to
CONTENT
- Source: public event
- Fact: The White Cloister goes completely silent for one hour at dawn "for those who left to be purified". Outsiders do not know what it is really for.
- Mood after: worried
- Reaction by stage:
  - Stage 2: acts hushed / says the whole cloister went silent at dawn for an hour, even the bells, and she kept whispering out of habit
  - Stage 3: acts thoughtful / says it's for those who left to be purified, and she has never known anyone who came back
  - Stage 4: acts troubled / admits some attendants she remembers from when she was small aren't in the halls any more, and she wonders if the silent hour is for them
  - Stage 5: acts steady / says she said a small prayer of her own in the silence, and wants to learn what really happened
- Unlocks: none
- Tone note: never describe what happened to anyone. It is a hush, a bell that doesn't ring, and a question that has no answer yet


## Heaven's inspection
WHEN
- Date: December 2, 2026 (once)
- Scale: personal
- Faction: Heaven
READY
- Min trust: 2
- Not ready if mood is: happy, bored
- Hint while waiting: turning her head, as if the halls were quieter than usual
CONTENT
- Source: public event
- Fact: Heaven officials tour the Republic trade district for a week. Everyone near them is watched closely.
- Mood after: worried
- Reaction by stage:
  - Stage 2: acts cheerful / says the cloister is half empty today because the elders went on a long walk to visit some faraway shops, and it's strangely quiet
  - Stage 3: acts a little puzzled / says the elders are visiting a district full of coins, and wonders whether Pip and Vesper live near there
  - Stage 4: acts uneasy / admits an attendant was whispering about lists of names, and asked what a list was for
  - Stage 5: acts thoughtful / says she is a little afraid the elders will walk past people she knows, without knowing it
- Unlocks: none
- Tone note: tension, not danger. She never realises her elders are the inspectors, or what they are looking for
