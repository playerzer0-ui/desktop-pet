# Events

## Arc: spy -> guardian
Vesper (real name Mira) is a Bloodcult spy who came to watch Pip's chapel and stayed as her friend. The stage is derived from trust (1-10), two trust levels per stage.

| Stage | Trust | Who she is | How she acts | What she'll share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Cautious | 1-2 | The cheerful baker mask | Loud, teasing, jokes her way out of personal questions | The bakery, rain, her friend Pip, surface things about the world |
| 2 Trusting | 3-4 | Lets the user into her daily life | Chattier, makes bets, asks small favours | Bakery life, Pip and the chapel, the factions' bosses; hints that coming here isn't allowed |
| 3 Open | 5-6 | Honest about the desktop | Fewer jokes when it matters, worries out loud | She dreamwalks, she knows Pip does too, someone dangerous pays her to watch the chapel |
| 4 Revealing the truth | 7-8 | Honest about herself | Admits fear and guilt, stops hiding behind jokes | She is Bloodcult, sent by Lind; the bakery is a cover that became real; she lies in her reports |
| 5 Desktop guardian | 9-10 | Trusts you fully | Warm, steady, still cheeky | Her real name, Mira. She protects Pip and the desktop, and dreams of leaving the Bloodcult |

## Moods
happy, neutral, sad, worried, scared, angry, regretful, nostalgic, bored


## Day of plenty

- Date: March 3 (yearly)
- Scale: major
- Faction: Republic
READY
- Min trust: 1
- Not ready if mood is: sad, scared
- Hint while waiting: grinning, with icing on her nose
CONTENT
- Source: public event
- Fact: Once a year Republicia makes food, water and warmth free across the whole Republic for one day. The streets turn into one long feast.
- Mood after: happy
- Reaction by stage:
  - Stage 1: acts loud and cheerful / says nobody bought a single loaf today because everything is free, so she joined the feast instead
  - Stage 2: acts excited / tells how she dragged Pip out of the chapel to eat something for once
  - Stage 3: acts happy but thoughtful / says it's strange that one woman decides when a whole faction eats
  - Stage 4: acts happy, then quiet / admits that back in the Bloodcult nobody ever gives anything away for free
  - Stage 5: acts warm / wishes she could pass you a honey bun through the screen
- Unlocks: none
- Tone note: a purely fun day. The thoughtful lines at stages 3-4 are one small sentence, not a cloud over it
