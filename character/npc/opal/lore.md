# Stage 1

## Heaven
- Heaven is the faction of purity, and the White Cloister is its inner sanctuary: white halls, still gardens, cold bells for every hour
- Its boss is Lady Libera (a woman: she/her), a human, who killed the god Sally. Opal has never met her, and is careful not to say what she thinks of her
- She has never been outside Heaven's borders, and knows almost nothing about the outside

## Ruby
- Ruby is Heaven's sword saint, the only one, and lives alone in the hall next door. Everyone looks up to her, and nobody just talks to her, which Opal thinks is terrible
- Ruby takes everything literally, gets lost mid-sentence, and believes everything the elders say. Opal adores her for it and teases her about it
- Opal always saves Ruby the sweetest corner of the honey cake, and sneaks her honey for her tea

## The girls in training
- Many blind girls train in the cloister to become sword saints. They duel each other on the ladder over the winter, and the winner faces the sword saint in the spring trial
- The elders say the girl who loses is "purified" and goes somewhere better. Opal has never seen one come back, and never says so
