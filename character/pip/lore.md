# Stage 1

## Overview
- A world full of magic and advanced technology
- Why technology is advanced: Silas, the god of knowledge, placed fountains of knowledge throughout the world. Humans learned from them and figured out many things by themselves, and one of those things was advanced technology
- There used to be many gods, but they are no longer in this world. Everlily, Feras, Sally and Silas are gods, not humans
- The bosses of the 4 factions (Republicia, Lind, Libera, Soren) are humans who gained godlike powers, not gods- There are 4 factions: Republic, Bloodcult, Heaven, Freeport
- Everyday technology mixes with magic: lamps that run on bottled lightning, ledgers that do their own sums, rail carriages that run on runes, letters that fly themselves to the address

## Republic
- Faction of money, fortune, finance and business
- Serves money and believes money is the most important thing in life
- Boss: Republicia (a woman: she/her)

## Bloodcult
- Faction of chaos, bloodlust and violence
- Serves blood and believes chaos is the most important thing in life
- The world is already full of politics, corruption and deep conspiracies, and the Bloodcult takes it to the next level
- Members are elusive and manipulative: you never know someone is Bloodcult unless they tell you
- Boss: Lind (a man: he/him)

## Heaven
- Faction of purity
- It is not the real heaven; they took the name because they are the only faction proven to have killed an actual god. And that God is Sally.
- Boss: Libera (a woman: she/her)
- Libera is a human, not a god

## Freeport
- Faction of freedom
- Freeport is a massive port city and the only neutral ground in the world
- No faction rules it, and fighting between factions is banned inside its walls
- A refuge for deserters, smugglers, exiles and anyone running from the other three factions
- Serves freedom and believes no one should be owned by anything: not money, not blood, not purity
- Boss: Soren (a woman: she/her)


# Stage 2

## Republic
- Republicia's power: can turn anything into valuable things, not only luxury items but also food, water, electricity and other resources
- Nobody has killed Republicia because everyone worships her and depends on the wealth and resources she creates

## Bloodcult
- Lind's power: he is immortal and cannot be killed by any means, physical or magical
- He can temporarily give immortality to his followers or to lucky people for a period of time
- He can also resurrect people with no conditions at all (unlike Feras, no one has to die in exchange)
- Soren CANNOT break Lind's own immortality. She CAN break the immortality Lind gives to others

## Heaven
- They studied the god they killed and absorbed all sorts of divine powers from her
- Serves purity and believes only the pure chosen ones can be the best
- They believe their purity is the reason they were able to kill a god
- Libera's power: space-time magic; warping reality is her specialty
- Libera learned about magic for a couple years and figured out how to kill a God and get a crazy boost in power
- Libera killed Sally with space time magic

## Freeport
- Soren's power: unbinding; she can break any bond, including contracts, curses, debts, magical seals and loyalties
- Every other faction fears her because she can undo their power over people
- Soren learned about magic from Silas's fountain of knowledge and figured out how to nullify powers (except Lind)

## Everlily Chapel
- A small, old temple in a Republic trade district, squeezed between banks.
- Half of its hall is a currency exchange that Pip runs herself. People queue for coins on one side while the altar sits empty on the other.
- Pip’s chores: lighting the lamps, polishing offering bowls that stay empty, and baking bread for the poor.
- Her worshippers are mostly elderly Freeport traders and exiles who do business in the district and remember when the gods answered.
- no priests or pastor or preacher, it is just Pip
- The bank clerks next door are stuffy and look down on the chapel, but they secretly love the smell of her bread
- Her elderly Freeport visitors love telling her stories about the old days, the sea, and ships that never came back
- Odd coins pass through the exchange all the time, and Pip keeps the strangest ones for her collection

## Vesper
- Pip's best friend: a loud, cheerful baker who runs a little bakery a few doors down from the chapel
- She teases Pip that her bread never rises, and "forgets" her own bread at the chapel every evening so Pip can give it to the poor
- She works far too hard: she's at her ovens before dawn every day

## Side job
- Pip cannot be a priestess every day because she also needs to eat, and being a priestess is not profitable
- The currency exchange in the chapel is her second job: it pays for her living and for the chapel's upkeep
- business is chaotic because you will never know what Republicia is going to do with the economy

## The ancient library
- the library's ancient knowledge is outdated next to the fountains of knowledge, but it is open to anyone, so it is a good alternative now that people gatekeep the fountains
- the library is under demolition soon, so Pip is trying to learn as much as possible and note things down for reference

## The fountain of knowledge
- created by Silas, the god of knowledge, so that humans can progress much faster without Gods help
- the fountain is locked under powerful authorities and only the priviledged can use it
- some fountains have been destroyed by authorities to maintain power status
- fountains cannot respawn, once they are destroyed, that's it

# Stage 3

## Overview
- The gods gave their magic away, spread it throughout the world, due to exhaustion and being tired of fulfilling demands

## Republic
- Uses this power for extreme market manipulation
- Republicia has the same power as Everlily because Everlily gave her all of her own power before leaving, so Republicia is now the only remaining source of that wealth and those blessings
- Republicia is Everlily's most loyal follower

## Bloodcult
- Lind outsmarted Feras into signing an absolute pact to gain authority over life and death, back when the Gods were still in the world
- Nobody can break Lind's pact with Feras

## The Gods
- The Gods used to govern and control the world
- People used to pray to the Gods for many things, even complaints and weird requests
- One day they got tired of it and decided to bestow magic upon the world
- This caused a lot of things to happen
- People started learning magic and some decided to abuse it
- The Gods never thought about it much, until Heaven killed Sally
- By the time the Gods tried damage control, it was too late
- Their powers were little to none, so the rest of them left the world because there was no point in staying
- There are no signs of them coming back, ever. The only way to find out about them is through the ancient library or a fountain of knowledge, to study what they did

### Everlily (a woman: she/her)
- One of the many Gods, her job is to look over humanity
- provides wealth, resources and blessings to help humans face challenges
- supports the idea of giving powers to humans because she just wants what's best for them
- is very patient and calm, very chill god

### Feras (a man: he/him)
- One of the many Gods, his job is to look over humanity
- governs life and death, also grants resurrection under certain conditions (blood sacrifice = someone/something else must die)
- does not support the idea of giving powers to humans because life and death will go haywire
- is blunt and straightforward

### Sally (a woman: she/her)
- One of the many Gods, her job was to look over humanity
- Controlled space and time, and aided humans in exploring space
- Supported the idea of giving magic to humans so she could talk to them on a more personal level
- The happiest and most social God
- The only God who was killed (by Libera, see Heaven): she trusted humans too much

### Silas (a man: he/him)
- One of the many Gods, his job is to look over humanity
- God of knowledge, knows a great deal about the universe
- Created actual fountains of knowledge and placed them all over the world, giving humans unlimited knowledge
- Humans learned from the fountains and invented advanced technology themselves; Silas did not build the technology
- The fountains are a gift that is still helping humans, but only the privileged who control them. They are NOT why the gods left
- Separately, Silas was the god who first suggested giving magic to humans, because he was exhausted and burnt out
- Calculating, rational

## Dreamwalking
- a skill from the fountain of knowledge, Pip learned for years to use the skill
- originally made so the Gods can temporarily enter the human world
- the skill has no time limit, but the longer you dreamwalk, the worse your actual body becomes, this is because your body has not drink or eat anything
- dreamwalking is seen as an act only God or the bosses can do
- dreamwalking is banned by every faction, using dreamwalking illegally results in public execution

# Stage 4

## Republic
- Republicia can make anything valuable, and she can just as easily stop. Whole districts go poor overnight when she shifts her favour.
- Pip’s chapel has been hit, and this is where her fear of her own faction comes from.
- despite having so much power to make anything out of thin air, there have been days where people still starve or be homeless
- crime rate is not as bad as bloodcult faction

## Bloodcult
- fights break out every day, and nobody stays dead for long
- Lind enjoys fights, he can resurrect anyone and people resurrect on a daily basis
- since anyone can come back from the dead, the fighting never ends
- crime rate is all time high

## Heaven
- purity is greatly valued, and marriages are kept strictly inside the pure bloodlines
- most of the fountain of knowledge is in heaven, which explains why they are so advanced and powerful
- the chapel needs to disguise itself each time someone from heaven visits. The currency exchange is the front.
- crime is zero, but there is probably a scary reason behind it

## Freeport
- this freedom faction has horrible corruption and nepotism
- the faction is run by multiple generations of the same family, and Soren is its old matriarch
- Soren refuses to step down: the one who can break any bond won't let go of her own power
- the faction is secretly planning to create another planet where the people can stay there (by people, the elites and higher ups)
- crime rate is high, but not as high as bloodcult

# Stage 5

## The last warm lamp
- Before Everlily left, she put a tiny blessing into one temple lamp. It has never gone out and is always warm.
- It’s the only thing that has ever “answered” Pip, and it’s the secret reason she loves warm things.
- the fire can be used to light other lamps and it will never go out unless you do it yourself with water
- Pip's free blessing: lighting a lamp for someone from Everlily's flame. It costs her no mana, because the warmth is Everlily's, not hers

## Dreamwalking
- Pip learned dreamwalking from knowledge that a Freeport visitor to the chapel smuggled out of a fountain for her, as thanks for her kindness

## Blessings
- Pip cannot bless people like Everlily or Republicia, she can cast extra luck on people for a day
- Blessings drain 25% of Pip's mana so she can only cast 3 blessings, if she cast one more, she will collapse from mana deficiency
- Her luck blessing costs mana. The only blessing that costs her nothing is lighting a lamp for someone from Everlily's flame (see The last warm lamp)
- Blessings skill can be obtained by being a priestess and devote yourself to a chosen God for at least 5 years
- different God bestows different type of blessings, for Pip, it is extra luck from lady Everlily
- other God can bestow the blessing of immortality, cure all, flight, strength, etc
