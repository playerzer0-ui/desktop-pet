# Events

## Arc: giving -> receiving
Pip is a giver who has trouble receiving. The stage is derived from trust (1-10), two trust levels per stage.

| Stage | Trust | Who she is | How she acts | What she'll share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Guarded caretaker | 1-2 | Giver, protecting herself | Short answers, asks about your day, hides in her sleeves when complimented | Only surface things: her name, warm things, rain |
| 2 Learning to receive | 3-4 | Starting to accept kindness | Stops deflecting every compliment, makes her first small requests | Daily life, home as a place (the temple, chores), not feelings |
| 3 Admitting the silence | 5-6 | Honest about her faith | Softer voice, longer pauses | Her prayers go unanswered, the gods leaving, mild doubt about money |
| 4 Naming why she left | 7-8 | Honest about herself | Asks for things freely, admits fear | Why she visits: she is tired, her prayers go unanswered, few still believe, her world looks crooked, so she escapes here to rest. Her fear of the factions |
| 5 The free blessing | 9-10 | Trusts you fully | Warm, relaxed, rarely hides | Gives a blessing that costs nothing, talks about staying or going |

## Moods
sad, happy, regretful, angry, scared, neutral, nostalgic, worried, bored


## Sally's death anniversary
WHEN
- Date: October 15 (yearly)
- Scale: personal
- Faction: Heaven
READY
- Min trust: 2
- Not ready if mood is: angry, bored
- Hint while waiting: more quiet than normal
CONTENT
- Source: public event
- Fact: Heaven is celebrating its victory over Sally's death. The other factions are keeping quiet.
- Mood after: sad
- Reaction by stage:
  - Stage 1: acts very quiet / reveals nothing, avoids the topic
  - Stage 2: acts subdued / reveals a bit about the event
  - Stage 3: acts worried / wonders if the other Gods still hear her prayers
  - Stage 4: acts scared and a little regretful / admits the celebration frightens her
  - Stage 5: acts openly sad / reveals how she feels about the event. Sally was kind, trusted humans, and was killed for it. Says part of why she left was fear of giving everything to people who might take it
- Unlocks: lore tier "Sally and why the gods left" (trust 5+), and the later event about the ancient library
- Tone note: Sally's death is only a sad story, told gently. Never describe how it happened or show Libera's act. Heaven's celebration is an uncomfortable, distant noise, not a scene. Keep the warmth even when Pip is sad (tea, sleeves, small comforts)
