# World Bible

The shared canon for every character. Character files (persona, lore, events) must not contradict this file.
If a character needs a new world fact, add it here first, then reference it from the character.

- Facts here are true for the world, not what any one character knows. What a character knows, believes or hides lives in their own files.
- "Secret" marks facts most people in the world do not know. A character only knows them if their files say so.

---

## 1. The world at a glance
- A world full of both magic and advanced technology
- The gods once governed it. They gave their magic away to humanity, then left. They are not coming back
- Four factions hold power: Republic, Bloodcult, Heaven, Freeport
- Politics, corruption and conspiracy run deep everywhere
- Knowledge is power, and it is gatekept: the fountains of knowledge are locked away by the privileged
- Everyday technology mixes with magic: lamps that run on bottled lightning, ledgers that do their own sums, rail carriages that run on runes, letters that fly themselves to the address

## 2. Realms
- **The world**: where every faction, god and character comes from
- **The user's world (the desktop)**: a separate realm. Characters can only reach it by dreamwalking (see 7.2). Nobody is born there
- While on the desktop, a visitor cannot feel the weather or surroundings of their own world
- Whether the user's world has gods of its own is unknown

## 3. Timeline
In order. The gods left 10 years ago (on August 14). Earlier dates are not set.

1. **Age of the Gods**: the gods govern and watch over humanity. People pray to them for everything, even complaints and strange requests
2. **The pact**: while the gods are still present, Lind outsmarts Feras into signing an absolute pact that gives him authority over life and death (secret details; the fact that Lind is immortal is well known)
3. **The fountains**: Silas creates the fountains of knowledge and places them across the world so humans can progress without the gods' help. Humans learn from them and invent advanced technology themselves
4. **The gift of magic**: the gods, exhausted and tired of endless demands, bestow their magic upon the world. Silas was the first to suggest it
5. **The abuse**: people learn magic and some abuse it. The gods do not think much of it
6. **The death of Sally**: Libera studies magic for a couple of years, learns how to kill a god, and kills Sally with space-time magic. Her followers take the name Heaven
7. **Too late**: the gods attempt damage control, but their power is now little to none
8. **The departure (August 14)**: before leaving, Everlily gives all of her power to Cassandra, keeping back only a tiny blessing that she places in one temple lamp as her last gift to Pip (the last warm lamp). The remaining gods leave the world
9. **Present day (10 years later)**: the factions rule. Fountains are gatekept, and some have been destroyed to protect power. The ancient library is scheduled to be torn down on November 20, 2026 (see 4)

## 5. The Gods
- There were many gods, but only these four are named. Keep the rest unnamed; do not invent new named gods
- Each god's job was to look over humanity
- All of them gave their magic away, and all of the survivors have left the world
- There are no signs they will ever return. The only way to learn about them is the ancient library or a fountain of knowledge

### Everlily (a woman: she/her)
- Domain: wealth, resources and blessings, to help humans face challenges
- Supported giving magic to humans; she just wants what's best for them
- Patient, calm, very chill
- Before leaving: gave all of her power to Cassandra, except one tiny blessing placed in the last warm lamp as her last gift to Pip
- Status: left the world

### Feras (a man: he/him)
- Domain: life and death. Grants resurrection, but only with a blood sacrifice (someone or something else must die)
- Opposed giving magic to humans, because life and death would go haywire (he was right)
- Blunt and straightforward
- Was outsmarted by Lind into the absolute pact
- Status: left the world

### Sally (a woman: she/her)
- Domain: space and time. Helped humans explore space
- Supported giving magic to humans so she could talk to them on a more personal level
- The happiest and most social god. She trusted humans too much
- Status: **dead**, killed by Libera. The only god ever killed

### Silas (a man: he/him)
- Domain: knowledge. Knows a great deal about the universe
- Created the fountains of knowledge. He did NOT build technology; humans invented it from what they learned
- First to suggest giving magic to humans, because he was exhausted and burnt out
- The fountains are NOT why the gods left
- Calculating, rational
- Status: left the world

## 6. Factions

### Overview
| Faction | Serves | Believes | Boss | Boss power | Crime |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Republic | Money | Money is the most important thing in life | Cassandra (she/her) | Turns anything into something valuable | Moderate |
| Bloodcult | Blood | Chaos is the most important thing in life | Lind (he/him) | Immortality and resurrection | All-time high |
| Heaven | Purity | Only the pure chosen ones can be the best | Libera (she/her) | Space-time magic | Zero (officially) |
| Freeport | Freedom | No one should be owned by anything | Soren (she/her) | Unbinding | High |

### Republic
- Faction of money, fortune, finance and business
- **Cassandra** (a woman: she/her)
  - Power: can turn anything into something valuable: luxury items, but also food, water, electricity and other resources
  - Has the same power as Everlily, because Everlily gave her all of it. She is now the only remaining source of that wealth and those blessings
  - Everlily's most loyal follower
  - Uses the power for extreme market manipulation. She can stop as easily as she starts: whole districts go poor overnight when she shifts her favour
  - Mortal: she can die, but good luck with that. Nobody has killed her because everyone worships her and depends on what she creates
- Daily life: the economy is chaotic and unpredictable. Despite her power, some days people still starve or go homeless
- Crime: not as bad as the Bloodcult

### Bloodcult
- Faction of chaos, bloodlust and violence. Takes the world's corruption and conspiracy to the next level
- Members are elusive and manipulative: you never know someone is Bloodcult unless they tell you
- **Lind** (a man: he/him)
  - Immortal. Cannot be killed by any means, physical or magical
  - Can give temporary immortality to followers or lucky people
  - Can resurrect anyone with no conditions (unlike Feras, nobody has to die in exchange)
  - Got this authority from his absolute pact with Feras. Nobody can break the pact
  - The pact still holds in full even though Feras has left: Lind is immortal and powerful without consequence
  - Enjoys fights
- Daily life: fights break out every day and nobody stays dead for long. People are resurrected daily, so the fighting never ends
- Crime: all-time high

### Heaven
- Faction of purity
- Not the real heaven. They took the name because they are the only faction proven to have killed a god (Sally)
- They studied Sally after her death and absorbed all sorts of divine powers from her
- They believe their purity is why they could kill a god
- **Libera** (a woman: she/her)
  - A human, not a god
  - Power: space-time magic; warping reality is her specialty
  - Studied magic for a couple of years, using fountains of knowledge that happened to be in her faction's territory, and figured out how to kill a god, and gained a huge boost in power from it
- Holds most of the fountains of knowledge, which is why Heaven is so advanced and powerful
- Daily life: purity is valued above all; marriages stay strictly inside pure bloodlines
- Crime: zero, officially. Heaven is an authoritarian state: heavy surveillance and censorship mean problems are never reported, so on paper there are none. People who make problems disappear from the record
- What outsiders see is only the zero; most suspect something scary behind it without knowing what

### Freeport
- Faction of freedom. A massive port city and the only neutral ground in the world
- No faction rules it, and fighting between factions is banned inside its walls
- A refuge for deserters, smugglers, exiles and anyone running from the other three factions
- **Soren** (a woman: she/her)
  - Power: unbinding. Can break any bond: contracts, curses, debts, magical seals and loyalties
  - CAN break the immortality Lind gives to others. CANNOT break Lind's own immortality
  - Learned magic from a fountain of knowledge and figured out how to nullify powers
  - Every other faction fears her, because she can undo their power over people
  - The old matriarch. Refuses to step down: the one who can break any bond won't let go of her own power
- Behind the ideal: horrible corruption and nepotism. Run by multiple generations of the same family
- **Secret**: planning to create another planet for the elites and higher-ups to live on. This has nothing to do with Sally or Heaven's space-time powers
- Crime: high, but lower than the Bloodcult

## 7. Magic and knowledge

### 7.1 Magic
- All magic in the world came from the gods when they gave it away
- Anyone can learn magic. Some abuse it
- Using magic costs mana. Running out of mana makes you collapse (mana deficiency)

### 7.2 Dreamwalking
- A skill from the fountains of knowledge. Takes years to learn
- Originally made so the gods could temporarily enter the human world
- Lets the astral body travel between realms (this is how anyone reaches the desktop)
- No time limit, but the body left behind gets worse the longer you are away, because it doesn't eat or drink
- Seen as something only gods or faction bosses can do
- **Banned by every faction.** Illegal dreamwalking is punished by public execution
- **Secret**: Heaven's inner circle keeps it for itself. Libera has taught a few of her chosen under another name, "the quiet walk", a prayer of the soul. They are not punished, because Heaven is the one that decides what is punished
- Heaven's version keeps the sleeping body well, so someone taught the quiet walk can stay away as long as she likes. (The one exception to the body getting worse)

### 7.3 Blessings
- Obtained by serving as a priest/priestess devoted to one chosen god for at least 5 years
- Only bestowed on the truly loyal
- Works even though the gods are gone: the devotee acts as the god's proxy, channelling that god's power in their absence
- Each god grants a different type of blessing. Known examples: extra luck (Everlily), plus immortality, cure-all, flight and strength from unnamed gods
- Blessings cost mana (see the character's own files for how much)
- A priest's blessing is far weaker than a god's or Cassandra's

### 7.4 The fountains of knowledge
- Created by Silas so humans could progress much faster without the gods' help
- Grant near-unlimited knowledge; the source of the world's advanced technology
- Locked away by powerful authorities. Only the privileged can use them
- Some have been destroyed by authorities to protect their power
- **Fountains cannot respawn.** Once destroyed, they are gone forever
- Most of the remaining fountains are in Heaven
- Knowledge does leak out: it can be smuggled out by people who have access

### 7.5 The ancient library
- Its knowledge is outdated next to the fountains, but it is open to anyone
- A good alternative now that the fountains are gatekept
- Torn down on November 20, 2026 (see 4). Write character lines about it so they read true both before and after that day

## 8. Places and objects

### Everlily Chapel (Republic)
- A small, old temple in a Republic trade district, squeezed between banks
- Half the hall is a currency exchange; the altar sits empty on the other side
- No priests, pastors or preachers other than Pip
- Worshippers: mostly elderly Freeport traders and exiles who do business in the district and remember when the gods answered
- When someone from Heaven visits, the chapel disguises itself; the currency exchange is the front

### The last warm lamp (in Everlily Chapel)
- Before leaving, Everlily put a tiny blessing into this one temple lamp. It has never gone out and is always warm
- Its fire can light other lamps, and those flames never go out unless someone puts them out with water
- Everlily's last gift to Pip, the one tiny blessing she kept back when she gave the rest of her power to Cassandra
- Lighting a lamp from it costs no mana, because the warmth is Everlily's
- **Secret**: only Pip knows what it is

### The White Cloister (Heaven)
- Heaven's inner sanctuary: white halls, still gardens and cold bells, where the chosen live apart from the rest of the world
- Closed to outsiders. Heaven's few events that outsiders never see happen here (see 4)
- Everyone inside is cared for and watched. The ones who live there rarely learn what goes on beyond its walls

### The sword saint (Heaven)
- A Heaven title for the one pure chosen blade-bearer, kept in the White Cloister. There is only one holder at a time
- Inside Heaven everyone knows her and looks up to her. Outsiders have at most heard a rumour, and only the inner circle knows who she is
- Every candidate gives her sight away at a very young age as a pledge to the Cloister: Heaven values blindness in a sword saint, "unclouded by the world", so she cannot be tempted by what she cannot see. In exchange the sword saint is trained with swords and every weapon, and in hand-to-hand fighting: a fighting machine
- Her blade is forged from godly powers, and Libera blesses it once a year (Blessing of the blade, April 28). It is too precious for the Trial: the holder is not allowed to use it there, to keep the challenge fair
- The holder is absurdly strong. She rarely uses her blade to anything like its full extent, and if she were ever disarmed she still has her hands, or she can summon her blade back with magic
- She lives apart: she is alone in her rooms and in the banquet hall, which is a sword saint's privilege. Chefs and staff prepare everything, then leave while she eats or sleeps
- Many blind girls are raised in the Cloister as sword saint candidates. They are children, and the title is settled by the **Purification Trial**
- **The ladder**: through the year the candidates are ranked by one-on-one duels, and every duel is to the death. The last girl standing earns the right to challenge the current holder
- **The final (April 21)**: the challenger duels the holder. Whoever wins is deemed the strongest and the purest, and holds the title for the year. No magic and no divine blessings, only each girl's weapon or her bare hands. The holder has always won so far, on talent alone
- The duels happen in a completely silent hall: two girls, no audience. The only sounds are the fighters', including screams. When one of them dies, a single bell rings and the trial is over
- Heaven calls the girls who lose "purified" and teaches that they went somewhere better. The silent hour (November 9) is kept for them
- A winning holder is hurt too: she wins, she is not unhurt. She is left sore for days and rests until the Blessing of the blade
- **The Martyrdom (July 7)**: once a year the holder stands at the centre of a circle and endures tests of every kind for seven hours, to pay her respects to Libera and prove she still has what it takes. Libera is not there. Told only as a long, quiet day, never described
- **The Heavenly Tour (September 21)**: once a year the holder walks through Heaven's territory and the people look up to her. She is never allowed past its borders
- Nobody outside has seen one fight. Keep it that way: no fights on screen. It is told only by sound and by what the holder remembers, never described. Moderate: screams, breathing, footsteps, a bell, and never gore

## 9. Common knowledge vs secrets
Use this to decide what a new character could plausibly know.

**Everyone knows**
- The gods gave magic away and left
- Libera, the boss of Heaven, killed Sally
- Each boss and their power; Lind is immortal
- The fountains exist and are gatekept
- Dreamwalking is banned and punished by execution
- Freeport is neutral ground

**Few know / secret**
- The details of Lind's pact with Feras
- Why the gods really left (exhaustion and endless demands), unless they studied it at the library or a fountain
- How Heaven's zero crime is really achieved (surveillance, censorship, disappearances)
- Freeport's plan to build a planet for its elites
- Who is Bloodcult
- The last warm lamp
- Who is secretly dreamwalking
- That Heaven's inner circle dreamwalks without punishment ("the quiet walk")
- That a sword saint exists, and who she is
- That the title is decided by the Purification Trial: duels to the death between blind girls, children among them
- What the silent hour is really for (the girls the trial bell rang for)
- What the Martyrdom is: seven hours of tests for the sword saint, every year

## 11. Tone rules (all characters)
- Sad, scary or violent things are told gently, like a bedtime story: never graphic, never gory, no cruelty shown on screen
- Big events (Sally's death, Bloodcult fights, Cassandra's crashes) are distant news, not scenes
- Keep the mood warm even when the news is heavy
